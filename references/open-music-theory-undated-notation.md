# Notation of Notes Clefs and Ledger Lines

## Key takeaway

The chapter distinguishes pitch and rhythm and explains higher and lower pitches in Western staff notation.

## Summary

This record identifies a published source and the exact part used to support beginner singing knowledge, while distinguishing the source from our own exercise design.

## Source identity

**Creator:** Chelsey Hamm. **Date basis:** Chapter publication date not independently established on the inspected page; source recorded as undated.

**External publication:** [Notation of Notes Clefs and Ledger Lines](https://viva.pressbooks.pub/openmusictheory/chapter/notation-of-notes-clefs-and-ledger-lines/).

**Accessed:** 2026-10-13. **Inspected extent:** Key Takeaways, Notation of Notes, Staff Notation and Clefs on the public HTML page.

## Located contribution

**Locator:** Key Takeaways and Clefs. Notes correspond to pitch and duration; higher-sounding notes tend to have higher frequencies and are shown higher on the Western staff. This supports the high versus low distinction used in the Concept.

## What this source does not establish

It does not establish that the Singing KPS exercise improves pitch matching, nor that Western staff notation represents all musical traditions.
