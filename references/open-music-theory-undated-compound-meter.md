# Open Music Theory on compound meter

## Key takeaway

Three subdivisions within a beat and the number of beats in a group are distinct dimensions of metre.

## Summary

The chapter supports the compound-versus-simple comparison in the rhythm and meter Model. It does not prescribe the timing or accent of every performance bearing a particular signature.
