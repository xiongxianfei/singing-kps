# Open Music Theory on pitch and octaves

## Key takeaway

A pitch label includes octave placement, whereas pitch class groups octave-related notes.

## Summary

Hamm and Hughes, American Standard Pitch Notation, Open Music Theory, undated. [Original chapter](https://viva.pressbooks.pub/openmusictheory/chapter/aspn/); the [credited LibreTexts republication](https://human.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Music/Music_Theory/Open_Music_Theory_2e_%28Gotham_et_al.%29/01%3A_Fundamentals/1.06%3A_American_Standard_Pitch_Notation_%28ASPN%29) supplied the inspected sections on 2026-10-14.

## Located contribution

American Standard Pitch Notation and Pitch versus Pitch Class distinguishes an individual pitch from its pitch class. ASPN and Octave Designations explains letter and octave-number labels, including C4. These are notation conventions, not recommended notes for a particular voice.
