# Rockschool Vocals Grade 1 repertoire

## Key takeaway

The official list includes Stand by Me by Ben E. King in Grade 1 vocal repertoire; suitability still depends on the arrangement and singer.

## Summary

[RSL Awards' Vocals Grade 1 page](https://www.rslawards.com/products/vocals-grade-1/) identifies educational arrangements and accompanying resources. It supports a repertoire-selection rationale, not a claim that every beginner can sing the original recording comfortably.

## Located contribution

The repertoire list names Ben E. King and Stand by Me. Product features describe arrangements and backing tracks. The Key & Range erratum includes this song and reports an octave correction to notation; that is an additional reason not to infer a personal key or range from a title or grade alone.

## Application and limits

The song is used as an authored example for learning and performing a whole song. No purchased arrangement, notated pitch range, exact key, accompaniment or vocal demonstration was inspected. The grade does not validate our Practice, prescribe a starting key or establish learner competence. The commercial recording and the educational arrangement must not be assumed identical.
