# Measured source and filter in a physical vocal tract

## Key takeaway

The experiment separately measures excitation, tract transfer and output under controlled physical-model conditions.

## Summary

The [author-hosted article](https://www.phys.unsw.edu.au/jw/reprints/Source-Filter.pdf), [DOI record](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40857-016-0046-7), supports a bounded source-filter representation rather than an inverse diagnosis of a singer.

## Located contribution

The Introduction and Figure 2 relate glottal-flow excitation, vocal-tract transfer and radiated pressure. The terminology footnote distinguishes resonance from spectral-envelope formant terminology. The local Model uses this separation and explicitly states the independence approximation.

## Inspection and limits

The identified passages and figure were inspected on 2026-10-14. Detailed calibration and raw experimental data were not independently audited. A controlled physical tract does not establish every interaction in a living voice or a universal teaching method. The local normalised-gain table is synthetic, not copied data. Neither a spectral peak nor the simple product rule identifies a unique healthy vocal configuration. The source figure is not reproduced.
