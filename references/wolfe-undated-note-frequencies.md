# UNSW note frequencies and ratios

## Key takeaway

Doubling frequency gives an octave, and a declared tuning convention converts note labels into reference frequencies.

## Summary

## Located contribution

The page explains octave frequency ratios and twelve equal semitone steps. With MIDI number m and A4 at 440 Hz, frequency is 440 times 2 to the power ((m - 69) / 12). Its cents equation compares pitch by logarithmic frequency ratio. These expressions support our synthesized reference targets and optional measurement explanation.
