# NATS undergraduate voice pedagogy syllabus

## Key takeaway

This syllabus supplies an established subject outline for voice pedagogy.

## Summary

The [NATS PDF](https://www.nats.org/_Library/Science_Informed_Voice_Pedagogy_Resource/Voice_Pedagogy_Syllabus_for_a_Two-Course_Curriculum-First_Semester.pdf) informs the [singing subject map](../docs/subject-map.md). It is a teacher-education syllabus, not evidence that a particular beginner exercise works.

## Located contribution

Page 1 identifies voice function and teaching outcomes. Pages 2-3 locate breathing, phonation, registration, acoustics, articulation, perception, voice health and learning within the course. These topic distinctions support the map's voice-pedagogy coverage.

## Adaptation and limits

The map selects and regroups subjects for beginner orientation. It does not adopt the university timetable, assessments or teacher-qualification scope. The syllabus does not supply the missing local explanations, diagnose a voice or validate our task sequence. Linked textbooks and teaching resources were not inspected for this record. Publication date remains unknown; the access date is not a publication year.
