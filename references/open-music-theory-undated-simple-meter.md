# Simple Meter and Time Signatures

## Key takeaway

The chapter describes a beat as the recurring pulse that listeners can tap.

## Summary

This record identifies a published source and the exact part used to support beginner singing knowledge, while distinguishing the source from our own exercise design.

## Source identity

**Creator:** Mark Gotham, Chelsey Hamm and Kris Shaffer. **Date basis:** Chapter publication date not independently established on the inspected page; source recorded as undated.

**External publication:** [Simple Meter and Time Signatures](https://viva.pressbooks.pub/openmusictheory/chapter/simple-meter-and-time-signatures/).

**Accessed:** 2026-10-13. **Inspected extent:** Terminology and listening discussion on the public HTML page.

## Located contribution

**Locator:** Terminology. The chapter uses tapping along with a recurring pulse to illustrate a beat and introduces how beats group into meters.

## What this source does not establish

The explanation is not a scientific demonstration that tapping improves singing, nor a mandate to use a four-beat group.
