# Wolfe and colleagues on singing mechanics

## Key takeaway

The chapter connects respiratory energy, vocal-fold oscillation and acoustic shaping while recognising source-tract interaction.

## Summary

The [author-hosted prepublication PDF](https://www.phys.unsw.edu.au/jw/reprints/AIRSchapter.pdf) supplies selected physical explanations, not a complete or individually validated singing programme. The version is recorded as undated rather than assigning an inferred publication year.

## Located contribution

PDF page 2 explains the voice source through airflow-supported oscillation and mechanically adjusted conditions. Pages 2-3 distinguish source harmonics from vocal-tract resonances and describe interaction. These passages inform the respiratory phonatory and source-filter Models.

## Inspection and limits

Pages 1-3 and their images were inspected on 2026-10-14. The illustrations are not reproduced. The later discussions of registers and resonant strategies were not reviewed for inclusion, so this record does not establish those outline topics as implemented. The selected account does not prescribe a beginner's pressure, airflow, vowel adjustment or vocal range. Constructed quantities in the local Models are not measurements from this chapter or evidence of safe technique.
