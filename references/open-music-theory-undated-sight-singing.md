# The Basics of Sight Singing and Dictation

## Key takeaway

The chapter provides educational strategies for isolating pitch and rhythm challenges during sight singing.

## Summary

This record identifies a published source and the exact part used to support beginner singing knowledge, while distinguishing the source from our own exercise design.

## Located contribution

**Locator:** Strategies for Sight-Singing and Sight-Counting. The chapter suggests slower tempo, breaking a melody into shorter sections, tapping the beat and practising pitch without the original rhythm or rhythm without its pitches when one part is difficult.

## What this source does not establish

Sight singing conventionally assumes reading notation, while this KPS exercise requires none. The cited chapter does not test the effectiveness of this exact beginner sequence or certify vocal safety.
