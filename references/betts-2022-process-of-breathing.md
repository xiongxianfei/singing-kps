# OpenStax on respiratory mechanics

## Key takeaway

Pressure, flow, resistance and respiratory movement are related but distinct parts of an introductory breathing account.

## Summary

The [official chapter](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/22-3-the-process-of-breathing) supports selected respiratory relationships in the respiratory phonatory Model. Its general physiology is not a singing-specific control strategy.

## Located contribution

Section 22.3 distinguishes pressure relationships, factors affecting flow and ventilation movements. It relates flow to pressure difference and resistance and explains the roles of movement and recoil. These premises support separating air quantity from its driving conditions.

## Inspection and limits

The named HTML sections and book citation information were inspected on 2026-10-14. Clinical tests, linked activities and numerical lung-volume examples are not adopted. A steady-flow simplification cannot simulate the time-varying glottis or supply a learner's safe breathing target.
