# Pfordresher and colleagues on singing accuracy

## Key takeaway

The paper proposes connected perceptual and motor functions rather than one universal cause of singing errors.

## Summary

Pfordresher and colleagues (2015), Music Perception 32(3), 227-231, DOI 10.1525/mp.2015.32.3.227. [Author-hosted article](https://mindlab.research.wesleyan.edu/files/2014/01/Pfordresher-et-al-2015-theory.pdf), inspected 2026-10-14 as recorded in metadata.

## Located contribution

Figure 1 and pages 228-229 connect auditory perception, sensorimotor translation, motor planning and auditory feedback. Categorical pitch and long-term memory supply additional relations. The framework distinguishes these functions and their connections as possible contributors to performance.

## Application and limits

The local Model explains a selected functional loop in prose and a table. It does not copy the figure, claim a complete neural account, diagnose an individual or prove an exercise's effect. Discussion of briefly retaining the target and a constructed learner case are our task-level applications, not newly measured components. The paper is a theoretical introduction, not a trial of this KPS Practice.
