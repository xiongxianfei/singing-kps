# Taking Care of Your Voice

## Key takeaway

NIDCD explains vocal fold vibration and describes voice warning signs and general protective habits.

## Summary

This record identifies a published source and the exact part used to support beginner singing knowledge, while distinguishing the source from our own exercise design.

## Source identity

**Creator:** National Institute on Deafness and Other Communication Disorders, National Institutes of Health. **Date basis:** The page displays a Last Updated Date of June 11, 2025; its publication text also says Updated March 2021. The filename uses the page update year, not a claim that the publication originated in 2025.

**External publication:** [Taking Care of Your Voice](https://www.nidcd.nih.gov/health/taking-care-your-voice).

**Accessed:** 2026-10-16. **Inspected extent:** Official HTML warning signs and Use your voice wisely; the official-page search excerpt supplied What is voice and both update dates. The prior 2026-10-13 inspection covered the three sections listed in the metadata.

## Located contribution

**Locator:** What is voice describes airflow, vibrating vocal folds and sound shaping; How do you know when your voice is not healthy lists hoarseness, strain and effort; Use your voice wisely advises against singing when hoarse or tired and against range extremes. These are general health guidance, not individual medical clearance.

## What this source does not establish

It does not prescribe the Singing KPS stage sequence, diagnose the reader, identify an optimal starting pitch, or quantify the effects of the exercise.
